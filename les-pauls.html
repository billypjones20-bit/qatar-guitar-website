About Les Paul Guitars

The Gibson Les Paul is a solid-body electric guitar that was introduced in 1952. It features a single-cutaway body design, mahogany body with maple top, and humbucking pickups.

Key Features

  • 🎸 Solid mahogany body with maple top
  • 🎸 Humbucking pickups for rich, warm tone
  • 🎸 Long-lasting sustain
  • 🎸 Classic single-cutaway design
  • 🎸 Versatile for rock, blues, jazz, and more

Why Choose a Les Paul?

Les Paul guitars are renowned for their rich, warm tone and exceptional sustain. Used by legendary guitarists like Jimmy Page, Slash, and Eric Clapton, the Les Paul is a timeless instrument that delivers powerful, versatile sound for any genre.
